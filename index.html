How to Play AFTERECHO

1. The Player

You control the cyan avatar. Navigate across the grid to reach the emerald exit beacon. Use WASD, Arrow keys, or swipe on mobile.

2. The Temporal Echo

Every few moves, an Echo of your past self materializes. It replays your exact sequence of past moves step by step.

3. Avoid Paradoxes

You cannot step onto the same tile as an Echo or cross paths directly. Breaking causality causes a Temporal Paradox!

4. Switches & Pressure Plates

Pressure plates open doors while held down. Coordinate so your Echo steps on a plate while you slip through the open door!

5. The Wait Action

Press Spacebar, Z, or the Wait button to pass a turn without moving. This lets your Echo catch up or hold plates in place.
