How to Play AFTERECHO

1. Direct Tap or Keys

Control the cyan avatar to reach the emerald exit beacon. Click/tap any adjacent tile, swipe on mobile, or use WASD / Arrow keys.

2. The Temporal Echo

After the level's delay, an Echo materializes at your start position and replays your exact past moves step-by-step.

3. Avoid Paradoxes

Never collide with an Echo, swap tiles head-on, or trap an Echo behind a closed gate. Breaking causality triggers a Temporal Paradox!

4. Plates, Toggles & Blocks

Square plates open gates while held down by you, an Echo, or a heavy block. Circular toggles flip gates ON/OFF each time anyone steps on them.

5. Wait & Smart Hints

Tap your own avatar or press Spacebar to Wait 1 turn. Stuck? Click 💡 Hint to highlight the optimal next move!
